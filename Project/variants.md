# Variants

> Assigned Week 05. Until then this table is a placeholder.

Each student gets one facade strip of the base building: an elevation, a floor
range, and the programme behind it. Everyone solves the same problem; nobody
solves the same instance of it.

The strips are **not** equally difficult, deliberately. A south-facing strip in
front of open-plan studio has an obvious shading problem. A north strip behind a
stair core barely has a problem at all, and part of your work is arguing for
what it should do instead. Judging what your strip actually needs is the first
design decision you make.
