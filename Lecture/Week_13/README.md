# Week 13 — A hanging canopy: form-finding

🔹 **Standalone build**

Kangaroo: form as a consequence of forces, and how to read a solver that will not settle.

## What this session covers

- relaxation as a method, not an effect
- anchors and springs; reading a solver that will not settle
- form as a consequence of physical constraints

## In the room

**Today's goal:** A fabric canopy over the courtyard: a mesh hung from four posts, relaxed into equilibrium, then inverted into a compression shell.

We build it together, live, from the data flow in [`goals.md`](/Lecture/Week_13/goals.md). At **3 critical points** I stop and you vote on what we place next (🗳️ in `goals.md`, polls run on `TBC-POLL-TOOL`), then we argue about the result before anyone touches the canvas. See [how the builds work](/README.md#how-the-builds-work).

You will not be given a `.gh` file. [Reading one teaches nothing, and the building is the skill](https://kb.rccn.dev/computation/rhino-grasshopper/grasshopper/describing-a-definition).

## Homework
