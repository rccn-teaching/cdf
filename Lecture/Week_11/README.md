# Week 11 — Panelisation and subdivision

Dividing a surface without it dividing you.

## What this session covers

- `Lunchbox` and friends; quad, triangular, diamond, hexagonal
- planarity: why a quad on a double-curved surface is not flat, and the escapes
- panel numbering and keeping identity through the pipeline
- the seam and the pole — where a surface betrays you

## In the room

**Three goals**, each stated as a data flow — see [`goals.md`](/Lecture/Week_11/goals.md).
Built together, then in pairs, then alone. Goal 3 is the one that does the
work; see [the format](/README.md#three-goals).

You will not be given a `.gh` file. [Reading one teaches nothing, and the
