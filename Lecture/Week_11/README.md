# Week 11 — Panelisation

🧱 **Class build** · part 3 of 5

The class facade is cut into panels that could be made, and each panel gets an identity that survives to the cut file.

## What this session covers

- quad, diamond and triangular panels
- planarity: quads on a double-curved surface are not flat
- panel numbering: identity through the pipeline
- the seam and the pole: where a surface betrays you

## In the room

**Today's goal:** The strip surface panelised; every panel numbered; non-planar panels flagged in red before anyone orders glass.

We build it together, live, from the data flow in [`goals.md`](/Lecture/Week_11/goals.md). At **3 critical points** I stop and you vote on what we place next (🗳️ in `goals.md`, polls run on `TBC-POLL-TOOL`), then we argue about the result before anyone touches the canvas. See [how the builds work](/README.md#how-the-builds-work).

You will not be given a `.gh` file. [Reading one teaches nothing, and the building is the skill](https://kb.rccn.dev/computation/rhino-grasshopper/grasshopper/describing-a-definition).
