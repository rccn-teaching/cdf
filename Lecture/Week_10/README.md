# Week 10 — A perforated screen: attractors and fields

🔹 **Standalone build**

Distance as a driver, remapping, and the question every attractor has to answer: is the variation doing anything?

## What this session covers

- distance as a driver: point, curve and surface attractors
- `Remap Numbers`: name both domains out loud
- shaping a falloff deliberately with `Graph Mapper`
- is the variation *doing* anything?

## In the room

**Today's goal:** A laser-cut screen: a grid of holes whose size follows distance to an attractor, with a minimum web between holes that the laser can actually cut.

We build it together, live, from the data flow in [`goals.md`](/Lecture/Week_10/goals.md). At **3 critical points** I stop and you vote on what we place next (🗳️ in `goals.md`, polls run on `TBC-POLL-TOOL`), then we argue about the result before anyone touches the canvas. See [how the builds work](/README.md#how-the-builds-work).

You will not be given a `.gh` file. [Reading one teaches nothing, and the building is the skill](https://kb.rccn.dev/computation/rhino-grasshopper/grasshopper/describing-a-definition).

## Homework

- Choose a driver for your strip and defend it in two sentences: what does it respond to, and how would you show it is doing something?
