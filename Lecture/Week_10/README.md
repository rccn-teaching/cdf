# Week 10 — Attractors, remapping, fields

The first week the output looks like design, and the first week that is a trap.

## What this session covers

- distance as a driver; point, curve and surface attractors
- `Remap Numbers`, and naming both domains out loud
- `Graph Mapper`: shaping a response curve deliberately
- the honest question: is the variation doing anything, or does it just look busy?

## In the room

**Three goals**, each stated as a data flow — see [`goals.md`](/Lecture/Week_10/goals.md).
Built together, then in pairs, then alone. Goal 3 is the one that does the
work; see [the format](/README.md#three-goals).

You will not be given a `.gh` file. [Reading one teaches nothing, and the
building is the skill](https://kb.rccn.dev/computation/rhino-grasshopper/grasshopper/describing-a-definition).

## Homework

- Build all three goals
- Choose a driver for your strip, and be ready to defend it
