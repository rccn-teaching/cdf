# Week 09 — Curves and surfaces, parametrically

Evaluate, divide, reparameterise. UV as a way of thinking.

## What this session covers

- `Divide Curve`, `Evaluate Curve`, and what the `t` parameter is
- reparameterising, and why almost everything wants 0-to-1
- `Surface Box`, `Evaluate Surface`, and the UV grid — the Week 01 rectangle again
- frames and planes: orienting something to a surface without fighting it
- why trimmed surfaces behave oddly here — UV refers to the underlying patch

## In the room
